What the converter accepts

Decimal places
Two at most. An amount like 1.234 is refused, never rounded, so your figure does not change without you seeing it.
Size
Up to 30 digits before the decimal point, which reaches the octillions.
Formatting
Spaces, commas and a leading dollar sign are ignored. A leading minus gives an amount below zero.
Wording
Australian usage. 123 reads as ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THREE, and 1001 as ONE THOUSAND AND ONE.
